What we built

Runs on the robot today. Nothing faked.

Robot microphone

Raw 6-channel mic mixed to mono. No wake word; head touch wakes Noor.

Ghost bridge · ROS 2

Streams audio both ways, mutes the mic while Noor talks, reconnects on its own.

ElevenLabs agent

Speech-to-text, LLM and voice in one agent. English, Arabic, Hindi.

Robot speaker

16 kHz PCM straight to the AgiBot X2’s speaker.

Who’s talking?

The bridge infers the next speaker from whom Noor just addressed, labels the log, and passes it to the agent as a hidden note.

Three languages

Hindi that comes through in Urdu script is answered in Hindi, not Arabic.

Pure voice

No tools to wire on the robot. If the full bridge fails, a plain audio bridge takes over.

Open stack

Python + ROS 2 on the robot, TypeScript agent config, Next.js robot face.
